# Waffles

## Ingredients

- 2 tsp baking poweer
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 3/4 cups milk
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 3/4 tsp pure vanilla extract

## Preparation

1. Pre-heat the waffle maker
2. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt
3. Beat eggs thoroughly. Whisk in sugar, milk, and oil
4. Add liquid incredients to the dry incredients
5. Pour the batter into the waffle maker, do not overfill
6. Bake for about a minute or until golden brown
